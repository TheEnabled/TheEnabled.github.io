Neatly

A tranquil, atmospheric arrangement puzzle game focused on mindful object organization.

Role:
Gameplay Programmer & Level Designer
Engine / Tech:
Unity / C#
Genre:
Cozy / Sorting Puzzle
Platform:
PC / WebGL

What the Game is About

Neatly invites players into a calm, meditative space where chaos is turned into harmony. Players are presented with ceremonial and everyday items—delicate leaves, dried blossoms, hand-poured candles, and ornate brass trays—scattered across a mystical surface.

By discerning geometric patterns, color balances, and spatial relationships, players carefully position each item into its intended resting place, creating clean, satisfying visual symmetry and lighting ceremonial candles to complete each ritual.

How to Play & Controls

  • Mouse Left-Click & Drag: Pick up leaves, plates, and ornamental items to move them around the board.
  • Object Snapping & Stacking: Position objects over target slots or plates; releasing will snap them smoothly into valid orientations.
  • Match & Ignite: Click the ceremonial match to light candles once they are placed in their designated ritual positions.
  • Completion Condition: Once all objects are arranged harmoniously and all candles are lit, the scene activates with celebratory visual feedback and unlocks the next arrangement.

Sol's Key Contributions

Gameplay Programming

Engineered the 2D spatial drag-and-drop physics, raycast hit detection, dynamic sorting layers, and state validation scripts that verify proper object alignment.

Level & Puzzle Design

Authored puzzle layouts with progressive difficulty, designing visual cues and aesthetic guidelines that lead the player to organic "aha!" moments without intrusive tutorials.

Quality Assurance & Polish

Conducted extensive edge-case testing on overlapping collider bounds, mouse release behaviors, and audio-visual feedback to ensure smooth, bug-free playability.
