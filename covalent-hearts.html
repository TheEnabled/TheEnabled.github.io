Covalent Hearts

A witty, chemistry-themed narrative visual novel where the elements come alive.

Role:
Gameplay Scripting & QA Tester
Engine / Tech:
Ren'Py / Python / C#
Genre:
Visual Novel / Narrative Sim
Platform:
PC / Mac / Linux

What the Game is About

In Covalent Hearts, the periodic table of elements is transformed into an eccentric cast of characters navigating the pressures of high school chemistry and interpersonal attraction.

Players encounter characters such as Carbon—the notoriously versatile element struggling with the burden of bonding expectations—alongside a spectrum of reactive and noble personalities. Every conversation choice alters character affinity, leading players through branching narrative pathways, scientific wordplay, and heartfelt outcomes.

How to Play & Controls

  • Mouse Click / Spacebar: Advance dialogues, trigger scene transitions, and select on-screen narrative choices.
  • Dialogue Choices: Select conversation options that test emotional intuition and chemistry principles to raise affinity with specific characters.
  • Quick Controls Bar: Use on-screen buttons to access Back, History (log of past dialogue), Skip (speed through seen text), Auto, Save, Q.Load, and Preferences.
  • Multiple Endings: Replay or branch off previous saves to unlock all character routes and unique storyline conclusions.

Sol's Key Contributions

Systems & Scripting

Programmed dialogue condition flags, relationship affinity counters, and branching logic that dynamically determines character reactions and narrative routes.

UI/UX Implementation

Configured the custom dialogue box, text styling, character sprite transitions, expression shifts, and the quick navigation menu for seamless player comfort.

QA & Narrative Testing

Extensively regression-tested every story branch, verifying that save/load states preserve variables correctly and that no game-breaking dead ends or orphan flags occur.
