Paramnesia

A first-person psychological horror experience exploring distorted memories and atmospheric dread.

Role:
Gameplay Programmer & 3D Level Designer
Engine / Tech:
Unity / C#
Genre:
3D Psychological Horror / Thriller
Platform:
PC (Windows)

What the Game is About

Paramnesia places the player inside a claustrophobic, dimly lit residence where the boundary between memory and reality begins to fracture. Inspired by classic analog and psychological horror, the game builds unease not through cheap jump scares, but through eerie environmental shifts, unsettling visual compositions, and persistent audio tension.

As player memories distort, a solitary rotary telephone begins to ring relentlessly in the dead of night. Following the directive to "Answer the Telephone," players must explore the shadowy corners of the house, piece together fragmented recollections, and confront the truth lurking on the other end of the line.

How to Play & Controls

  • W, A, S, D: Walk through hallways and explore the interior environment.
  • Mouse: Look around, investigate surroundings, and direct focal attention.
  • Left-Click / E (Interact): Pick up the telephone receiver, open doors, examine paintings, and interact with environmental props.
  • Shift (Hold): Sprint away from sudden anomalies or quickly navigate between rooms.
  • Objectives: Pay close attention to on-screen prompts and audio cues (such as directional telephone rings) to progress through the story chapters.

Sol's Key Contributions

3D Gameplay Programming

Developed the first-person character controller, mouse smoothing, interaction raycast system, objective prompt manager, and event trigger system in Unity C#.

Level Design & Atmosphere

Architected the interior layout, mood lighting (low-key lighting with high-contrast shadows), prop placement, and pacing to maximize claustrophobic tension.

QA & Collision Polish

Executed thorough collision testing to eliminate player snagging on furniture, verified trigger zones, and optimized dynamic point lights for consistent frame rates.
